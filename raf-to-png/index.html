Why convert RAF to

RAF is Fujifilm's RAW format, and Fujifilm is the interesting outlier in the RAW world: since 2012 its X-series cameras use X-Trans sensors with a 6×6 color filter array that deliberately avoids the Bayer pattern, trading demosaicing simplicity for reduced moiré and stronger color fidelity.

That unusual sensor design is why RAF files need specific processing — generic converters that assume Bayer data render X-Trans files soft or artifacted. ShiftRaw's LibRaw engine includes proper X-Trans interpolation, so your files get the full-resolution treatment they deserve.

Converting RAF to PNG happens entirely in your browser: the decoder is WebAssembly, the camera's white balance is applied, and your photos never touch a server.

Best for: PNG is lossless — every pixel of the converted image is preserved exactly, with no compression artifacts, and it supports full alpha transparency. The trade-off is size: expect files 3–10× larger than an equivalent JPG. Choose PNG when quality is non-negotiable, when you need transparency, or when the image will be edited further before final delivery.

Frequently asked questions

Why do RAF files look different in different apps?
X-Trans sensors need dedicated demosaicing, and every renderer interprets them slightly differently. Fujifilm's own apps apply film simulations on top of raw data; ShiftRaw renders a clean, neutral interpretation with as-shot white balance — a solid base for your own grading.
Can this converter handle GFX medium-format RAF files?
Yes. GFX 50S/100S RAF files are large (100 MP from the GFX100 II) but supported — decoding just takes a few seconds longer. Memory-wise, the browser handles up to roughly 100 MP comfortably on a modern machine.
What are film simulations and do they survive conversion?
Film simulations like Classic Chrome or Acros are rendering profiles applied when Fujifilm software develops the RAF. The raw file stores only the tag of the selected simulation. ShiftRaw outputs a neutral rendering; for the simulation look, shoot JPG+RAW or use Fujifilm's app.
Why are RAF files bigger than same-megapixel Canon or Sony files?
Fujifilm stores the raw mosaic at 14-bit with lossless compression, and X-Trans's irregular pattern compresses less efficiently than Bayer. A 26 MP X-T4 RAF runs ~50 MB where a 24 MP Bayer RAW might be 25–35 MB.
When is PNG worth the larger file size?
When you need a pixel-perfect lossless copy, transparency, or plan to re-edit and re-save the file — PNG survives round-trips without quality loss. For straightforward photo sharing, JPG at high quality looks identical at a fraction of the size.
Is there a file size or batch limit?
No hard limits. Files are processed one by one entirely on your device, so the practical ceiling is your machine's memory - a 100 MP medium-format RAW decodes comfortably on any modern computer. Large batches simply queue and run sequentially.
