Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

Your photos never leave your device. All file conversion on ShiftRaw happens locally in your web browser. Your images are never uploaded to our servers, never stored, never processed remotely, and never seen by anyone but you. Once the page has loaded, you can even disconnect from the internet and the converter will keep working.

What we don't collect

  • Your photos — no upload, no temporary server-side storage, no queue.
  • No accounts: we don't have your name, email, or password, because there is no sign-up.
  • No file history: converted files exist only in your browser's memory until you download or close the tab.

What is collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics service may collect standard, non-identifying technical data when you visit: pages visited, approximate region, browser type, device type, and referral source. This data is used only in aggregate to understand traffic and improve the site. It is not linked to any personal identity, because we don't have one to link it to.

Advertising

ShiftRaw is free and supported by advertising. When ads are displayed, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Where required by law (including the EU/UK), a consent banner will be shown before non-essential cookies are set, and you may withdraw consent at any time.

Cookies

ShiftRaw itself sets no tracking cookies. Essential functionality of the site does not require cookies. Any cookies you encounter come from third parties (analytics and advertising) as described above.

Children's privacy

ShiftRaw is a general-audience tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date above will be revised and the changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the contact page.
