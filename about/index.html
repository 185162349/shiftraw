About ShiftRaw

A photo converter built on one stubborn principle: your photos are yours.

Why this site exists

Every photographer knows the ritual: a folder full of RAW files from a wedding, a trip, a product shoot — and a client, a platform, or a family group chat that only accepts JPG. The obvious solution is an online converter. The obvious problem with the obvious solution is that every mainstream one uploads your photos to a server first.

Sometimes that's fine. Sometimes those are client photos under contract, personal moments, or unreleased commercial work — and "fine" stops applying.

ShiftRaw exists because in 2026 this shouldn't be a compromise. Browsers are now capable computing platforms: WebAssembly runs the same open-source LibRaw engine that desktop RAW developers use, directly on your machine, at native speed. Your files can stay entirely in your hands — so they do.

How it works, honestly

  • The engine is LibRaw, compiled to WebAssembly and running on your CPU — the same decoding library behind many desktop applications.
  • Your files never leave your device. There is no upload endpoint, no temporary server storage, no queue. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works.
  • The site is free, supported by unobtrusive advertising. No accounts, no watermarks, no daily quotas, no premium tier holding the useful settings hostage.

Who makes it

ShiftRaw is built and maintained by a single independent developer who photographs badly but converts professionally. Bug reports, format requests and feature ideas are genuinely welcome — get in touch.
